Artisan Material Care

  • Carrara & Calacatta Marble: Never use acidic solutions. Clean only with neutral pH castile soap and micro-weave cotton.
  • Smoked Oak Flooring: Vacuum using felt-head attachment only; damp mop with organic beeswax emulsion every 3 weeks.
  • Unlacquered Brass Fixtures: Wipe with chamois cloth dry; celebrate natural patina development without harsh polishes.
  • Belgian Linen Drapes: Steam monthly with distilled water to release dust without dampening fibers.
